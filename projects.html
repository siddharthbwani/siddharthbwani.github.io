Dream Cricket 25

Jul 2025 -- Present | TestersHub

Served as the primary QA tester for Dream Cricket 25, a complex cricket game, focusing on manual testing for gameplay mechanics, UI/UX, and performance across mobile platforms.

  • Analyzed game requirements and design documents to create detailed test plans and test cases covering match modes, player actions, scoring, leaderboards, animations, and visual effects.
  • Performed functional, integration, smoke, exploratory, and ad-hoc testing to ensure gameplay stability and consistency across all scenarios.
  • Verified UI/UX elements such as menus, buttons, pop-ups, notifications, and in-game HUD for responsiveness and usability.
  • Conducted gameplay validation including player interactions, scoring logic, match progression, and controls to ensure bug-free experience.
  • Logged defects in JIRA with detailed reproduction steps, screenshots, severity, and priority; collaborated with developers for resolution.
  • Performed performance and stability testing to detect crashes, lag, and frame drops, ensuring smooth gameplay.
  • Participated in QA meetings, providing feedback to improve game quality and release readiness.
  • Contributed to a bug-free release of Dream Cricket 25, maintaining high standards of quality and player satisfaction.

This project strengthened my expertise in game QA, manual testing, and collaborative problem-solving, delivering a polished and stable product to end users.
